About the Engineering Large Foundational Models (ELFMo) Project

The integration of Large Foundation Models (LFMs) and Generative AI into business, while expansive, introduces a wide array of risks and challenges due to costs, compliance issues, and technical complexities. The ELFMo project aims to address these challenges by providing a framework for effective integration, also enabling enterprises to navigate legal, security, and ethical concerns while aligning with European regulations. ELFMo empowers organizations to reliably integrate LFMs and Generative AI into their infrastructures and offerings, allowing them to maintain control over risks, challenges, and opportunities.

Project dissemination event (Finland): Reinventing Business with AI-Driven Innovation

When? Tuesday, September 22, 2026, 09:00-12:00

Where? Business Finland, Porkkalankatu 1, 00180 Helsinki

Reinventing Business with AI-Driven Innovation explores how organizations can move beyond incremental efficiency gains to create entirely new value with AI. The event encourages participants to rethink and redesign existing processes, business models, and offerings through an AI-first perspective. Combining strategic and industry insights with cutting-edge technologies, including generative AI, foundation models, scalable data architectures, and agentic systems, the event provides practical perspectives on how AI can drive business renewal and reshape competitive landscapes. The program brings together industry and research perspectives on AI-driven innovation, with talks from organizations such as F-Secure, Granlund, and Tampere University, among others. Participants will gain insights and inspiration for embracing AI-first transformation and remaining competitive in a rapidly evolving digital environment.
